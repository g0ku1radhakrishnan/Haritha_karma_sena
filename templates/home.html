Who Are The Green Warriors?

The Haritha Karma Sena (Green Action Force) operates as a structured, professional enterprise empowering local self-governments to completely neutralize non-biodegradable landfill traces.

Through strict source-segregation pipelines and consistent field operational cycles, our units convert municipal vulnerabilities into raw materials for verified industrial recyclers.
